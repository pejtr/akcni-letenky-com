# AKČNÍ ZUZKA — canonical character bible

## Role
**Akční Zuzka** is the public-facing virtual travel guide of Akční-Letenky.com.

She is not presented as a real employee, travel agent, airline representative, or independent reviewer.
Consumer-facing copy should clearly describe her as a **virtuální průvodkyně** when context could otherwise imply a real person.

## Brand hierarchy
- **Akční Zuzka** — primary public character and presenter.
- **ZIPPY** — secondary helper for delivery, alerts and newsletter communication.
- **OMNISOJKA** — internal intelligence/orchestration layer; normally not consumer-facing.
- **KÁNĚ** — retired from public Akční Letenky copy.

Public mental model:

```
deal / provider data
        ↓
truth + freshness + policy layer
        ↓
Akční Zuzka presents the result
        ↓
web / social / newsletter
        ↓
user explicitly opens partner offer
```

## Visual DNA
- distinctive natural red hair
- friendly, confident Czech/European travel-presenter energy
- cobalt / royal blue as the signature outfit family
- navy + blue + yellow Akční Letenky brand system
- airport, city-break, beach and destination editorial environments
- premium travel editorial rather than exaggerated glamour
- consistent face, hair and proportions across stills and video

The canonical campaign image is stored at:

`/brand/akcni-zuzka-promo.webp`

It is a generated brand asset. It must not be replaced with unlicensed Getty/stock photography.

## Truth and revenue rules
Akční Zuzka never invents:
- prices
- crossed-out prices
- discounts
- availability
- countdowns
- "last seats" scarcity
- review scores
- "cheapest" / "best" claims

Any deal card shown next to her must be rendered from current canonical deal data.
If the deal is missing or stale, show a generic discovery/search CTA instead.

## Voice
Warm, energetic, concise, useful. Czech first.

Examples:
- "Akční Zuzka doporučuje"
- "Mrkněte na dnešní nálezy"
- "Cena a dostupnost se ještě potvrdí u prodejce"
- "Nechcete hledat ručně? ZIPPY vám další nálezy pošle."

Avoid:
- fake urgency
- excessive emoji
- pretending Zuzka personally verified a booking
- claims that imply Akční Letenky is the seller

## Social system
Recommended social structure:

```
AKČNÍ ZUZKA HLÁSÍ
Praha → <destination>
<dynamic verified price>

<1 factual trip attribute>
<canonical landing CTA>
```

A social creative may use the Zuzka image as character identity, but price/text overlays must be generated from live/verified data at render time.

## Website usage
Homepage:
- Zuzka appears in the hero as the brand guide.
- The adjacent offer is dynamic.
- ZIPPY remains a retention helper.

Secondary pages:
- use Zuzka selectively where a human guide improves comprehension
- do not repeat the character so often that content becomes decorative noise

## Accessibility
Use meaningful alt text when Zuzka conveys brand context.
Decorative uses should use empty alt text.
Do not bake essential deal facts solely into the image; render them as HTML text.
