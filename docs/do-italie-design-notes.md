# Design Notes from do-italie.cz/nabidky

## Card Layout
- Large image on left (about 40% width)
- Content on right (60% width)
- White background with subtle shadow
- Rounded corners

## Card Content Structure
1. **Location badge** - green pin icon with city/region name
2. **Star rating** - right aligned
3. **Title** - bold, large text (hotel name)
4. **Description** - gray subtitle text
5. **Features list** - checkmarks with icons:
   - Letenka z Prahy
   - Ubytování v hotelu
   - 3 dní
   - Pojištění zdarma
6. **Urgency timer** - yellow/orange badge "Tato cena platí ještě Xh Ym"
7. **Reviews** - 2-3 customer reviews with avatar, name, city, rating
8. **Price section**:
   - Original price (strikethrough)
   - Current price (large, bold)
   - "za osobu" label
9. **CTA button** - green "Vybrat termín" button

## Additional Elements
- "Právě se dívá X lidí" badge (top left of image)
- Discount badge "-30%" (top right of image)
- Social proof notification popup at bottom

## Filter Bar
- Destinace dropdown
- Termín (date picker)
- Délka (duration dropdown)
- Cena (price dropdown)
- Strava (meal plan dropdown)
- Počet osob (number of people)

## Colors
- Primary green: #22c55e (buttons, badges)
- Yellow/orange for urgency timers
- Gray for secondary text
- White cards on light gray background
